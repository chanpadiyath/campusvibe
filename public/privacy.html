Privacy Policy

INTERIM VERSION · LAST UPDATED 29 SEPTEMBER 2026

This is an interim version while CampusVibe is being tested. It describes what the site actually does today. A full version will be published before the public launch.

Who runs CampusVibe

CampusVibe is run by Chan, an individual. It is not connected to SRM Institute of Science and Technology.

What we collect and why

  • Your SRM email address. Used to check you have an @srmist.edu.in address and to send your 6-digit login code. The code expires after 10 minutes. We don't keep a list of users.
  • A login cookie. After you log in, your browser stores a cookie that contains your email address, protected against tampering, so you stay logged in for up to 30 days. Logging out deletes it.
  • Reports. If someone reports you, we store your email, the reason they chose from a fixed list, and the time. Reporters are stored as a scrambled ID, not their email. Reports are deleted after 30 days, and temporary bans are deleted when they expire.
  • Your chat choices. Video or text, and any interests you add, are kept in the server's memory only while you're connected. Interests you share with your match are shown to both of you.
  • Your IP address. Held in memory for up to an hour to stop abuse (for example, too many login attempts). Our hosting provider may also keep standard connection logs.
  • Your theme choice (dark or light) is saved only in your own browser.

What we don't collect

  • Messages pass through our server in real time and are not stored or logged.
  • Video and audio go between your device and your match's device (or through a relay server that can't see the content). We don't record them.
  • No analytics, no advertising trackers, and we don't sell your data.

Remember that the person you're chatting with could still screenshot or record the conversation.

Your IP address and video chats

While CampusVibe is being tested, video can connect directly between the two devices, which lets your match's browser see your IP address. Before the public launch we plan to route all video through a relay server so IP addresses stay hidden.

Other services we use

  • Google (Gmail) sends the login code emails, so your email address and code pass through Google.
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs (Cloudflare) deliver fonts and a code library, so they receive your IP address when the page loads.
  • A Google connection server helps set up video calls and sees your IP address.
  • The background video on the login page is loaded from an external video host.

Your choices

  • Log out at any time to delete the login cookie from your browser.
  • You can ask for your information to be deleted or corrected. Contact details will be added here before the public launch.

Changes

We'll update this page if anything changes, and publish a full policy before the public launch.
