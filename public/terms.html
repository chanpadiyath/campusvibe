This is an interim version while CampusVibe is being tested. A full version will be published before the public launch, and you'll be asked to accept it again.

1. What CampusVibe is

CampusVibe is a free website that randomly pairs people for video or text chats. It is an independent project, not a company. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to SRM Institute of Science and Technology. "SRM" is only used to describe who can sign in.

2. Who can use it

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • You must sign in with an email address ending in @srmist.edu.in that belongs to you.
  • Don't share your login or let anyone else use your account.

3. House rules

While using CampusVibe you must not:

  • show or share nudity, sexual content or anything sexual involving anyone under 18;
  • harass, bully, threaten, or post hateful content about anyone;
  • record, screenshot or share another person's video, voice or messages without their permission;
  • share other people's personal details (phone numbers, addresses, social accounts and so on);
  • spam, advertise, scam, or impersonate anyone;
  • do anything illegal, or try to break, overload or get around the security of the site.

4. Reports and bans

You can report the person you're chatting with. If several different people report the same account within a week, it is banned automatically for 24 hours. We may also ban accounts for longer, or permanently, for serious breaks of these rules. We may remove access at any time if we believe someone is putting others at risk.

5. Your safety

  • The people you meet are strangers, even if they have an SRM email. Be careful what you share.
  • Anyone you talk to could screenshot or record the conversation, even though it breaks these rules.
  • If someone is in danger or you see illegal content, contact the police or India's cybercrime helpline on 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in.

6. The service

CampusVibe is free and provided "as is" while it's being tested. It may be unavailable, change, or shut down at any time. We don't guarantee who you'll be matched with or how long chats will work. You're responsible for what you say and show in your chats.

7. Privacy

How your information is handled is explained in the Privacy Policy.

8. Changes
